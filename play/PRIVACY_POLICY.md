# Privacy Policy — Geo Pin Cam

_Last updated: 4 September 2026_

Geo Pin Cam ("the app") is a camera app that writes a GPS/location stamp onto the
photos and videos you capture. This policy explains what the app does with your
data.

## Summary

- The app does **not** have accounts, servers, analytics, advertising, or
  tracking of any kind.
- Your photos and videos are **never uploaded**. They are saved only to your own
  device, in the standard gallery location (`Pictures/Geo Pin Cam` and
  `Movies/Geo Pin Cam`).
- Your location is read only while the camera screen is open, and only so it can
  be written onto the shot.

## Information the app accesses

**Camera** — used to show the viewfinder and to capture the photo or video when
you press the shutter. Nothing is captured in the background.

**Microphone** — used only to record sound while you are recording a video. It is
optional: deny it and videos record silently. Audio is never captured while
taking photos or while the app is in the background.

**Location (precise or approximate)** — read while the camera screen is open so
that the coordinates, accuracy, altitude and address can be stamped onto the
image and stored in the file's metadata. The app does not use background
location.

**Photos and media** — the app saves the photos and videos it creates to your
device's shared media store and reads back the ones it created to show them in
its in-app gallery.

## Data that leaves your device

Your photos and videos are never transmitted anywhere.

Two optional features send your **coordinates only** (never the image) off the
device when you are online:

1. **Address lookup** — converting coordinates into a street address uses the
   Android system geocoder, which may contact Google's servers. Governed by
   [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
2. **Map thumbnail** — the small map picture on the stamp is a map tile requested
   from OpenStreetMap. Governed by the
   [OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).

You can turn the map thumbnail off in Settings. The app still stamps coordinates
normally when it is offline.

## Data retention and deletion

All data (photos, videos, settings) stays on your device. Uninstalling the app
removes its settings. Photos and videos you captured remain in your gallery until
you delete them yourself.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change.
